Frequently asked

Help with scenes, postcards, and purchases.

How do I make a postcard?

Choose a destination on the world map, open a scene, and tap pieces in the tray to add them. Drag, rotate, or resize pieces until the scene feels right. Tap Done to save it to your in-app travel journal. You can revisit the steps in Settings → How to play.

Where are my postcards? Can I share one?

Open the travel journal and select a postcard. Its Actions menu lets you share, favorite, rename, edit, or delete it. Sharing opens your device's share sheet, where you choose the destination. Paper Worlds does not automatically upload the postcard to us.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Shop and tap Restore purchases while online. Use the same store account you used to buy the pack. If a purchase is still missing, email support with the product name and a description of the issue. Please do not send a full payment card number.

Is a purchase a subscription?

No. The destination packs, World Passport, and Support Paper are one-time purchases. World Passport unlocks current and future destination packs; Support Paper is a thank-you purchase and does not unlock destinations. Prices are shown by your app store before you buy.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your postcards and local progress are stored on your device. Deleting the app may remove them, and we do not have a server copy to restore. Restore purchases in Shop can recover eligible purchases associated with your store account, but it does not recover your postcards or progress.

Privacy

Postcards stay on your device; app usage and purchase information are handled by our service providers.
