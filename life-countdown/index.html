Frequently asked

Practical help for Life Countdown on iPhone.

What is Life Countdown?

Enter your birth date and an estimated life expectancy to see your life progress and countdown. Add milestones and loved ones, view your life in weeks, reflect in your journal, and read Stoic quotes. The countdown is a reflection tool based on your inputs, not a prediction of when you or anyone else will die.

How do I change my birth date or life expectancy?

Open Profile → Personal Settings. Update your birth date or life expectancy there. You can also manage your loved ones from Personal Settings.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is an optional one-time purchase through the Apple App Store. It unlocks unlimited loved ones, morning Stoic quote notifications, Life-in-Weeks themes, and milestones on your life grid. The App Store shows the current price in your currency before you confirm payment.

How do I restore Premium?

Open a locked Premium feature to show the upgrade screen, then tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase and connect to the internet. During onboarding, the upgrade screen also has a Restore button. Restoring a purchase restores eligible paid access, not your milestones, loved ones, or journal.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store billing and refunds. Visit Report a Problem to submit a request to Apple. For missing Premium access, try Restore Purchase first or email us.

How do I add a widget?

Open Profile → Widget Setup for instructions. On your iPhone, touch and hold the Home Screen, choose the option to add a widget, and search for Life Countdown. For a Lock Screen widget, touch and hold the Lock Screen, choose Customize, and select a Life Countdown widget. If the widget looks stale, open the app once to refresh its shared countdown data.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Open Profile → Notifications to enable and schedule your reminders. Also allow notifications for Life Countdown in iOS Settings and check whether Focus or notification delivery settings are silencing them. Morning Stoic quote notifications require Premium.

Where is my data stored? Can I use it on another device?

Your birth date, milestones, loved ones, journal entries, and preferences are stored locally on your device. No Life Countdown account or app-managed cloud sync is provided. We do not have a server copy to recover if you delete the app or lose your device. Device backups, if enabled, are controlled by your operating system and backup settings.

How do I delete my data?

Open Profile → Reset All Data and confirm Reset. This removes locally stored settings, milestones, loved ones, journal entries, and notification preferences and returns the app to onboarding. This does not refund or cancel an App Store purchase. Individual journal entries and milestones can also be deleted in the app.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Use Profile → Report a Bug or Request a Feature, or email the address above. Include your app version, device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Share screenshots only if you are comfortable including their contents; please omit private journal entries and other personal details.

Privacy

Your countdown and journal data stay on your device. Read the Life Countdown Privacy Policy for details about local storage, widgets, Apple services, and support messages.
