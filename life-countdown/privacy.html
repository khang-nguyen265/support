Life Countdown

Privacy Policy

Your time is finite. Make it count.

Life Countdown keeps your countdowns, milestones, loved ones, and journal on your device. Apple handles purchases, and we receive only what you choose to send us for support.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Data stored on your device

Life Countdown stores your birth date, chosen life expectancy, milestones and goals, loved ones’ names and birth years, journal entries and mood tags, notification preferences, and other app settings locally on your device. It uses this information to calculate life progress, show countdowns, and provide the features you choose. No account is required. We do not operate an app backend that receives this content or provides cloud sync. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your own backup settings.

Widgets and notifications

To display widgets, the app copies relevant countdown and milestone information into a shared storage area on your device that its iOS widget extension can read. This is on-device sharing between the app and its widgets, not an upload to us. Reminders, journal prompts, and Stoic quote notifications are scheduled locally after you grant notification permission. Their content, and widget content, may be visible on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. You can change notification permissions and preview visibility in iOS Settings.

Purchases and Apple services

Optional lifetime Premium access is purchased through the Apple App Store using Apple’s in-app purchase system. The app reads purchase and entitlement information to unlock Premium and restore purchases, and caches your Premium status locally. Apple handles billing; we do not receive your full payment card details. The app does not use RevenueCat. It also contacts Apple’s public App Store lookup service to check for newer app versions, sending the app’s bundle identifier rather than your birth date, journal, or milestones. As with other network requests, Apple receives connection information such as your IP address. Apple handles its services under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising, and diagnostics

The app does not integrate a third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK, and we do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. Apple may make platform diagnostics available according to your Apple privacy and analytics settings. This is separate from the personal content stored by Life Countdown.

Support messages

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and any app or device details you include. The in-app bug report draft includes the app version and platform details; you can review it before sending. We use support messages to answer requests and investigate issues, and retain them as needed for that purpose and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Email is processed by your email provider and ours. Please avoid sending private journal content or payment card details.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your settings and delete individual milestones, loved ones, and journal entries in the app. Profile → Reset All Data clears locally stored personal content and preferences and cancels scheduled reminders. Removing the app may also remove its local data. We do not have a server copy of that content to restore. Device backups are managed separately through your operating system. Resetting data or uninstalling does not erase Apple’s purchase records or refund a purchase; restoring purchases only restores eligible Premium access. To ask about or request deletion of support messages you have sent us, email khang.plant@gmail.com.

This support website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising, or contact form. GitHub may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the website under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Opening an email link uses your email app; it does not automatically send a message.

Children

Life Countdown is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a support message, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will publish the updated policy here and revise the date above.

Contact

Life Countdown is provided by Khang Nguyen. For privacy questions or support, email khang.plant@gmail.com or visit Life Countdown Support.
