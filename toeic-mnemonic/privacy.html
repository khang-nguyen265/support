TOEIC® Mnemonic

Privacy Policy

Your study data stays on your device.

TOEIC Mnemonic does not require an account. Your study progress and preferences are stored locally. Optional purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat; if you contact support, we receive your message.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Study data on your device

The app stores your learning progress, answers and review schedule, target score, daily word goal, language preference, onboarding state, and a cached purchase status on your device. Vocabulary content is bundled with the app. We do not operate an account or a server that receives or backs up your study history. Removing the app may delete this local data. Restoring a purchase does not restore study progress.

Purchases

The optional lifetime unlock is an in-app purchase processed by Apple through your App Store account. We use RevenueCat to check purchase status and restore access. RevenueCat may process an app user identifier, purchase receipts or transaction records, entitlement status, and device or app information needed for this service. We do not receive your full payment card number. See the Apple Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy for their practices. The app does not ask you to create an account with us.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

The app does not include an advertising or analytics SDK and does not use your study activity for cross-app advertising tracking. We do not sell personal information. Purchase processing still involves the information described above, so the app should not be described as collecting no data.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as your message, app version, and device details. We use this information to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. Email is handled by your email provider and ours. Please do not send a full payment card number or other sensitive information.

Retention and your choices

You can remove local study data by deleting the app. Purchase records may remain with Apple and RevenueCat for access restoration and recordkeeping. Support emails remain in our mailbox until no longer needed to handle the request. To ask about information associated with your support message or purchase identifier, or to request deletion where available, email khang.plant@gmail.com. We may need details to find the relevant record. Deleting the app does not automatically delete information held by Apple, RevenueCat, or email providers.

Children

TOEIC Mnemonic is designed for people preparing for the TOEIC exam and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us so we can review it.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical information when you visit them, such as your IP address and browser request data, under its privacy statement. This website does not use its own analytics or advertising scripts.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app or its data practices change. The date above will show the latest update. For privacy questions, email khang.plant@gmail.com.
