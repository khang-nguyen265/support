Privacy Policy

Fruitette

Fruitette collects nothing. There are no accounts, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers. Everything you do stays on your device.

Last updated: 15 July 2026

Information we collect

None. Fruitette does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contacts. Your game progress, unlocked fruit and boards, stars, and settings are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to us.

Photos

When you tap save on an arrangement you made, Fruitette writes that one image to your device's photo library. That is the only way the app touches your photos. Fruitette never reads, browses, scans, or imports photos you already have, and it does not send saved images anywhere.

Sharing

When you tap share, Fruitette hands the image to the standard iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The app does not upload your artwork to us or to anyone else. Whatever you pick in the share sheet is handled by that app or service under its own privacy policy.

Analytics & tracking

None. Fruitette contains no third-party analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no cross-app tracking. We do not build a profile of you, and there is nothing for us to sell or share.

Data storage

Game progress and settings are kept locally using your device's standard app storage. Deleting the app removes that data from your device. Because nothing is stored on a server, progress cannot be recovered once it is gone.

Children's privacy

Fruitette is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them. Since the app collects no information from anyone, this holds for players of every age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
